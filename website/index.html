Data: Sentinel-2 L2A, SEN2VENUS.

Sentinel-2 at 10 m, resolved to 2.5 m, with the doubt left visible.

Tessera sharpens medium-resolution imagery for field boundaries, narrow roads and small buildings. Each output ships with a georeference check and an uncertainty map, because some of the detail is inferred rather than observed.

Method

  1. Learn from real pairs1,000 Sentinel-2 and VENUS tile pairs from SEN2VENUS, four bands (blue, green, red, near-infrared), split 70/15/15 by image so the test tiles are never seen in training.
  2. Train a 2× SwinIRTransformer-based, L1 loss, 60 epochs. Each band is scaled by its own 99.5th percentile so near-infrared does not saturate.
  3. Run it twiceTiled inference with border trimming removes seam artifacts. The 2× model is applied twice: 10 m to 5 m to 2.5 m.
  4. Keep it a mapOutput is a float32 GeoTIFF in original units. CRS, pixel size, origin and footprint are checked against the input. On a test tile (EPSG:32631) the pixel size went from 10 m to 2.5 m with the origin unchanged.
  5. Show the doubtFour flipped copies of the input are run through the model. Where they disagree, the uncertainty map is bright.
  6. Pack it to travelWeights, band scales and settings are saved as one 11.7 MB model bundle, so the service starts without the training data.

Limits

5 m output
Validated. Compared pixel by pixel with real VENUS imagery on unseen tiles.
2.5 m output
Extrapolated. No real 2.5 m reference exists in the training data. We check only that averaging the result back to 10 m stays close to the input.
Uncertainty map
Computed for the 5 m stage only. It is a guide to where the model is unsure, not a confidence interval. Its link to real error is positive but weak (correlation 0.25 ± 0.13 over 20 test tiles).
Vegetation index
At 5 m, NDVI error is on par with bicubic (0.029 for both). Do not read the enhanced NDVI as more accurate; the measured gain is in structure, not in the index.
Fine detail
Edges and textures are model-inferred. Treat them as leads for analysts, not measurements.

Workbench

Upload a four-band Sentinel-2 GeoTIFF and the model returns a 2.5 m version. After a quiet period the first request can take a minute while the service wakes up.
